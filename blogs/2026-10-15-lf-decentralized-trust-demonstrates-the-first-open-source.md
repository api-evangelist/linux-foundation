Two LF Decentralized Trust (LFDT) communities—the ToIP / DIF Decentralized Trust Graph Working Group (DTGWG) and OpenVTC , an LFDT lab—reached a major milestone at the Linux Plumbers Conference and LF Open Source Summit EU in Prague last week: demonstrating the first verifiable trust community (VTC) for open source projects.
